Engineering assumptions & limitations

Reliable in this prototype: 2D dimensions when the part and reference lie on approximately the same plane, lighting is adequate, the reference is visible, and perspective is limited.

Not reliable: hidden depth, true 3D dimensions from one image, fine thread geometry, exact surface roughness, or aerospace-grade metrology.

Uncertainty model: approximately ±2 pixels of image-edge uncertainty is propagated through the detected mm/pixel scale. Perspective and lens effects are treated as additional qualitative limitations.
